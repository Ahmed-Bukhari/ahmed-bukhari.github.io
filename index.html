03About Ahmed

An engineer by training who moved from the plant floor into quality, consulting and governance, and now shapes policy at ministry level.

Ahmed Bukhari is a management consultancy and quality professional with experience across quality, compliance, risk, business processes, governance, operational excellence and health & safety management. He works effectively in high-pressure, complex environments, from a government ministry and a PIF company to consulting assignments that spanned MODON's headquarters and 35 industrial cities.

His path runs from process and quality control engineering, through regional quality assurance auditing and management consultancy, to GRC advisory and director-level roles at the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development.

2030

Professional objective. To apply his experience in quality, governance and operational excellence in contributing to Saudi Vision 2030.
